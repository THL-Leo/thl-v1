I started at Nuro in October 2025 as a Software Engineer.

The biggest change so far is how I work with multiple chat agents. I usually keep around 12 Claude/Codex sessions open in tmux, each managing its own worktree. I also built and use skills to standardize model behavior, because without clear guidelines it gets fuzzy between versions and even between sessions.

Multitasking got better — I can handle a lot more workload now, and I love trying new AI tools. Nothing really got harder; the tools help me understand new things faster.
